### **Solution: Classifying Differential Equations as ODE or PDE**  

To classify each equation, we check:  
- **Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs):** involve derivatives with respect to a **single** independent variable.  
- **Partial Differential Equations (PDEs):** involve partial derivatives with respect to **multiple** independent variables.  

---

#### **(a) $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} = 0$**  
- The equation contains **partial derivatives**: $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}$ and $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$.  
- The function $u$ depends on **two independent variables**, $t$ and $x$.  
✅ **It is a PDE (Partial Differential Equation).**  

---

#### **(b) $y'' - y' + y = x$**  
- The equation contains **only ordinary derivatives**: $y''$ and $y'$.  
- The function $y$ depends on a **single independent variable**, $x$.  
✅ **It is an ODE (Ordinary Differential Equation).**  

---

#### **(c) $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} = 0$**  
- The equation contains **partial derivatives**: $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$ and $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}$.  
- The function $u$ depends on **two independent variables**, $x$ and $y$.  
✅ **It is a PDE (Partial Differential Equation).**  

---

#### **(d) $y' + \sin(y) = x^2$**  
- The equation contains **only an ordinary derivative**: $y'$.  
- The function $y$ depends on a **single independent variable**, $x$.  
✅ **It is an ODE (Ordinary Differential Equation).**  

---

### **Final Answers:**
| Equation | Type |

| $\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} = 0$ | **PDE** |
